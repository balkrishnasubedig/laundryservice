About This Trek

The Khopra Ridge Trek (also known as the Khopra Danda Trek) is one of Nepal's most rewarding hidden gems — a community-managed trail in the Annapurna Conservation Area that sees a fraction of the visitors of Poon Hill or the main circuit, yet offers even more spectacular mountain panoramas.

The route climbs to Khopra Ridge (3,660m) and continues to the sacred high-altitude Khayer Lake (4,660m) — a holy lake revered by local communities, set in a dramatic alpine bowl beneath Annapurna South and the Nilgiri peaks. The views from the ridge stretch from Dhaulagiri (8,167m) in the west to the entire Annapurna massif in the east — arguably the finest unobstructed panorama of these two great mountain groups available anywhere.

The trail passes through genuine, unspoiled Magar and Gurung villages where tourism infrastructure is minimal — giving trekkers a taste of authentic rural Nepal rarely experienced on more popular routes.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1: Pokhara to Nayapul & Trek to Tikhedhunga (1,540m)

Drive 1.5 hours from Pokhara to Nayapul (1,070m). Begin trekking the well-known lower section through rice paddies and Gurung villages to Tikhedhunga.

~3 hrs trek1,540m

Day 2: Tikhedhunga to Ghorepani (2,860m)

Climb through the famous stone-stepped rhododendron forest to Ghorepani. A challenging ascent rewarded with magnificent views and warm Gurung hospitality.

~6 hrs2,860m

Day 3: Optional Poon Hill Sunrise, then Trek to Swanta (2,200m)

Optional pre-dawn hike to Poon Hill (3,210m) for sunrise over the Himalaya. After breakfast, leave the main Annapurna trail and head south on the less-traveled path toward Swanta village — a beautiful Magar settlement with no other trekkers.

Optional sunrise2,200m

Day 4: Swanta to Bayeli (2,310m)

Trek through quiet forest trails and agricultural terraces past Sinuwa and Kidu villages. The trail is less maintained but that's part of its charm — genuine solitude and wildflowers throughout.

~5 hrs2,310m

Day 5: Bayeli to Khopra Ridge Danda (3,660m)

A long, steep ascent through high forest and emerging alpine terrain. Arrive at the Khopra community lodge on the ridge with breathtaking 180° panoramic views of Dhaulagiri, Annapurna South, and Nilgiri. Sunset from here is exceptional.

~7 hrs3,660m

Day 6: Sunrise at Khopra Ridge, then Hike to Khayer Lake (4,660m)

Watch dawn break over the entire Annapurna–Dhaulagiri panorama. Then embark on the day hike to Khayer Lake (4,660m) — a sacred glacial lake at the foot of Annapurna South. The views along this high-altitude approach are extraordinary. Return to Khopra for dinner under the stars.

Lake Hike4,660m~7 hrs return

Day 7: Khopra Ridge to Chhomrong (2,170m)

Descend on the eastern side of the ridge through Dobato, passing through ancient forest with views of Machhapuchhre directly ahead. Arrive at the large Gurung village of Chhomrong — the gateway to the Annapurna Sanctuary.

~7 hrs descent2,170m

Day 8: Chhomrong to Nayapul & Drive to Pokhara

Final morning trek through terraced villages and forest back to the road head at Nayapul. Drive to Pokhara for a well-earned lakeside celebration dinner.

~5 hrs trekReturn

Days 9–10 (Optional): Pokhara Leisure

Optional extra days in Pokhara — paragliding, lake activities, World Peace Pagoda, Sarangkot sunrise, mountain flights, or simply unwinding by Phewa Lake.

Best Time to Trek

🍂 Autumn (Sep – Nov)

Optimal season. Clear views, stable weather, and the best conditions for the Khayer Lake hike. October is perfect — expect crisp, cold mornings and warm afternoons.

🌸 Spring (Mar – May)

Rhododendron blooms are extraordinary on this trail. Good mountain views and pleasant temperatures. Some cloud buildup in afternoons is typical.

❄️ Winter (Dec – Feb)

Khopra Ridge has snow and Khayer Lake may be inaccessible. The lower section is beautiful with snow-capped views. Suitable for experienced winter trekkers.

🌧 Monsoon (Jun – Aug)

Very wet lower sections with leeches. The ridge can have excellent views on clear days between monsoon clouds. Not generally recommended.

Important Notes

Community Lodges: Above Ghorepani, accommodation is in community-managed lodges that directly fund conservation and local livelihoods. Facilities are more basic than standard teahouses — embrace the experience.

Khayer Lake Hike: The hike from Khopra Ridge to Khayer Lake (4,660m) is a long day with significant altitude gain. Early morning departure is essential. Trekkers should feel well-acclimatized before attempting this extension.

Conservation Fee: A small additional conservation fee is paid directly to the Khopra Danda Community Management Committee — included in our pricing and directly supports trail maintenance and wildlife conservation.

Wildlife: This area is known habitat for the endangered red panda. Keep noise low and stay on trails — you may be rewarded with a sighting in the early morning hours.
