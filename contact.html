❓ FAQ

Common Questions

How far in advance should I book? +

For popular routes like EBC and Annapurna Circuit, we recommend booking at least 3–4 weeks ahead during peak season (Oct–Nov, Mar–Apr). Poon Hill and shorter treks can often be arranged with just a few days' notice.

What is included in the price? +

All packages include a licensed English-speaking guide, porter (up to 10kg), teahouse accommodation, all permits (TIMS, National Park, Conservation Area), airport transfers, and a first aid kit. International flights and personal gear are not included.

Do you offer custom itineraries? +

Absolutely! Every trek can be customised to your pace, interests, and schedule. We specialise in tailor-made itineraries and can combine trekking with paragliding, kayaking, or a Chitwan safari.

Is travel insurance required? +

Yes, travel insurance with helicopter rescue coverage is mandatory for all high-altitude treks (above 3,000m). We can provide recommendations for reputable insurance providers.
