Pokhara & Sarangkot Trek

Perfect for beginners - enjoy lakeside beauty and mountain sunrise views

Duration 3-5 Days
Altitude 1,592m
Difficulty Easy
Best Season Sep-May
Price From $299

About This Trek

Perfect for beginners and families! The Pokhara and Sarangkot trek combines the beauty of Nepal's second-largest city with stunning mountain views. Trek to Sarangkot viewpoint for breathtaking sunrise views over the Annapurna and Dhaulagiri mountain ranges. This trek is ideal if you have limited time but want to experience Nepal's natural beauty.

Detailed 3-5 Day Itinerary

Day 1: Kathmandu to Pokhara

Drive from Kathmandu to Pokhara (6 hours) via scenic mountain roads. Explore the beautiful Lakeside area with cafes and shops. Visit Fewa Lake for boating in the afternoon. Overnight in Pokhara.

Day 2: Pokhara to Sarangkot

Trek from Pokhara to Sarangkot (2-3 hours). This moderate hike takes you through local villages and farmland. Enjoy panoramic mountain views and explore the viewpoint.

Day 3: Sarangkot Sunrise Trek

Wake early for sunrise view over the Himalayas. Trek back and explore Pokhara. Optional activities include paragliding or helicopter tours.

Day 4-5: Optional Extensions

Extend with boating on Fewa Lake, visits to Devi Falls, and Davis Falls. Visit local museums and cultural sites.

Best Time to Visit

September to November: Clear skies, perfect visibility, cool temperatures. Ideal for trekking.

March to May: Rhododendrons bloom, warm days, and stunning views.

Perfect For

This trek is ideal for first-time trekkers, families with children, senior citizens, and anyone with limited time. No prior trekking experience necessary.
