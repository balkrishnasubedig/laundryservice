Langtang Valley Trek

Discover pristine alpine meadows and meet the friendly Tamang communities

Duration 7 Days
Altitude 3,430m
Difficulty Moderate
Best Season Mar-May, Sep-Nov
Price From $599

About This Trek

Escape the crowds on this scenic trek through the pristine Langtang Valley. Trek through alpine meadows, dense forests, and meet the warm Tamang people. This trek is known for its wildlife, including red pandas and Himalayan black bears, and its stunning mountain scenery. The Langtang Valley is less crowded than other popular treks but equally spectacular.

Detailed 7-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Kathmandu to Syabru Besi

Drive to Syabru Besi (6 hours). Trek along the Langtang River through forest trails. Experience your first night in the mountains.

Day 2-3: Syabru Besi to Langtang Village

Trek through rhododendron forests and alpine meadows to Langtang Village (3,380m). The trail becomes steeper but rewards with magnificent views.

Day 4: Langtang Village to Kyanjin Gompa

Reach the sacred Kyanjin Gompa monastery at 3,430m with panoramic views of Langtang Lirung and surrounding peaks.

Day 5: Kyanjin Gompa Acclimatization

Optional trek to Tsergo Ri (4,949m) for stunning sunrise views. Visit the local cheese factory and explore the valley.

Day 6-7: Return Trek

Trek back to Syabru Besi and drive to Kathmandu. Reflect on your mountain adventure.

Wildlife & Nature

Flora: Rhododendron forests, alpine meadows, wildflowers in season.

Fauna: Red pandas, Himalayan black bears, musk deer, pheasants, eagles, and other birds.

The Langtang National Park is a biodiversity hotspot with unique alpine ecosystem.

Best Time to Visit

March to May: Rhododendrons bloom, clear skies, temperatures warming up.

September to November: Clear views, cool weather, excellent hiking conditions.

Cultural Experience

The Tamang people are friendly and welcoming. Our guides can share stories about Tamang culture, traditions, and lifestyle. Experience authentic mountain hospitality and learn about their unique way of life.
