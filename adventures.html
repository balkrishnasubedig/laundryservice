🪂 Paragliding

Soar Above the Himalayas

Take flight from Sarangkot Hill and glide over the legendary Pokhara valley with sweeping 360° views of Annapurna, Machhapuchhre (Fish Tail), and the sparkling Phewa Lake below. Our tandem paragliding sessions are run by certified CIVL-licensed pilots with thousands of flights' experience.

No experience required. Flights last 20–30 minutes and depart multiple times daily, weather permitting.

🛶 Kayaking

Paddle Sacred Waters

Beginners are warmly welcome on the lake; experienced paddlers can challenge themselves on technical river sections with our professional instructors alongside.

🚠 Ziplining

Nepal's Most Thrilling Zipline

The Pokhara zipline is one of Asia's longest and steepest — stretching over 1.8km at speeds up to 120km/h. You'll race Superman-style over terraced fields and dense forest canopy with the entire Annapurna Himalayan range as your backdrop. The adrenaline rush lasts seconds; the view is unforgettable.

The entire experience from platform briefing to landing takes about 1.5 hours and is suitable for all experience levels.

🦏 Chitwan Safari

Into the Jungle Kingdom

Chitwan National Park — a UNESCO World Heritage Site — is one of Asia's finest wildlife reserves. Home to one-horned rhinoceroses, Royal Bengal tigers, gharial crocodiles, Asian elephants, and over 500 bird species, it offers a thrilling counterpoint to the high mountains. Our 2–3 day safari packages include jeep safaris, canoe rides on the Rapti River, elephant bathing experiences, and Tharu cultural village visits.
