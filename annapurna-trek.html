Annapurna Circuit Trek

The legendary circuit around Annapurna massif - 16-19 days

Duration 16-19 Days
Altitude 5,416m
Difficulty Hard
Best Season Sep-Nov, Mar-May

About This Trek

The Annapurna Circuit is considered one of the world's greatest trekking routes. This legendary trek circumnavigates the Annapurna Massif, offering stunning mountain vistas, diverse cultures, and unparalleled natural beauty. You'll trek through subtropical forests, alpine pastures, and cross the high Thorong La pass at 5,416m. The trek showcases Nepal's incredible geographical diversity.

Detailed 16-19 Day Itinerary

Day 1-2: Kathmandu to Besisahar

Drive to Besisahar and trek through dense subtropical forests and green valleys. Experience the first contact with mountain landscapes.

Day 3-6: Besisahar to Chame

Trek through the scenic Marsyangdi Valley, passing villages and local settlements. The views become increasingly dramatic.

Day 7-9: Chame to Manang

Ascend into higher altitudes with panoramic mountain views and cultural experiences. Visit local monasteries.

Day 10: Manang Acclimatization

Rest day for acclimatization with optional exploration of the scenic Manang Valley.

Day 11-12: Manang to Thorong High Camp

Trek to the high camp in preparation for the challenging pass crossing.

Day 13: Thorong La Pass (5,416m)

Cross the legendary pass at 5,416m with 360° mountain panoramas. One of the most rewarding moments of your trekking career.

Day 14-16: Thorong La to Jomsom

Descend to Jomsom and explore this unique Thakali cultural region.

Day 17-19: Return

Fly back to Pokhara and drive to Kathmandu.

Weather & Best Time

September to November: Clear skies, warm days, cool nights. Excellent visibility and perfect trekking conditions.

March to May: Rhododendrons bloom, clear mountain views, and pleasant weather.

Important Notes

Fitness Level: Good physical fitness and altitude acclimatization experience recommended.

Thorong La Pass: Can be challenging due to altitude. Our guides monitor health closely.

Insurance: High altitude and emergency evacuation insurance strongly recommended.
